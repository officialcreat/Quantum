# Quantum Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to creating an open, respectful, and inclusive community for everyone contributing to Quantum (QTM).

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior:

- Respectful communication
- Constructive feedback
- Community collaboration
- Responsible security reporting
- Professional conduct

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment or discrimination
- Personal attacks
- Spam or malicious activity
- Publishing security vulnerabilities without responsible disclosure

## Enforcement

The Quantum Foundation reserves the right to remove or reject contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.
