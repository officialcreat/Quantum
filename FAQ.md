# Quantum Frequently Asked Questions

## What is Quantum?

Quantum (QTM) is an independent Layer-1 blockchain using Proof of Work consensus.

---

## What is QTM?

QTM is the native cryptocurrency of the Quantum network used for transactions, mining rewards and network fees.

---

## Is Quantum open source?

Yes. The blockchain core is publicly available on GitHub under the MIT License.

---

## Does Quantum have a premine?

No. Quantum launched with a fair Proof-of-Work distribution.

Premine: **0%**

ICO: **None**

Private Sale: **None**

---

## Which consensus does Quantum use?

Proof of Work (PoW).
